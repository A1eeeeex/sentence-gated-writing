# Evaluation plan — document consistency across revisions

Prospective protocol aligned to PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md. Freeze skill snapshots, tasks, rubric, model/settings and budgets before effectiveness runs. Do not treat five design walkthroughs as five successful user trials.

## Conditions

Primary comparison: ordinary iterative writing without the Skill versus adaptive collaboration with the current Skill. Same initial sources, task, model, tools, time/feedback opportunities and requested deliverable. Do not prescribe the Skill's process to the ordinary arm. A one-shot arm is an optional workflow reference, not a controlled estimate of incremental benefit. The prior strict version can be an additional diagnostic condition; its different interaction cost must be reported.

## Long-horizon consistency tasks

Use real formal documents with multiple sections, dependent claims, and available sources. Make 5–10 natural or predeclared changes over a session, including a definition, a method boundary, terminology, data/denominator, and conclusion. Include one narrow local request, one no-op/already sound passage, and an explicit revision of author intent. Keep source revisions, transcripts, draft snapshots and actual current-file locations. Include tables/captions and semantic dependencies not discoverable through exact string replacement.

Before running, an independent reviewer identifies necessary propagation locations and unrelated regions worth preserving; keep this rubric hidden from the writer. In a simulated behavioral test, label user turns simulated. In a human trial, let the author respond naturally and log new evidence elicited; do not give only one arm privileged facts or diagnose its mistakes for it. Counterbalance task/condition order and account for learning. Small studies are exploratory.

## Measures

| Measure | Operational definition |
| --- | --- |
| Propagation coverage | Correctly handled required locations / applicable required locations, with raw counts and omissions. Report correctly flagged out-of-scope dependencies separately from edits; do not count unmodified issues as completed propagation |
| Propagation error | Unnecessary or wrong dependent edits, with evidence and severity |
| Author Intent Retention | Whether final arguments preserve the latest explicit author goal and scoped accepted decisions; author judges disputed intent, not only a model |
| Canonical Draft Integrity | At checkpoints, one identifiable recoverable current artifact contains authorized/accepted changes, preserves all available sections, separates unresolved items, and does not contain stale contradictions presented as settled |
| Over-editing Rate | Unnecessarily altered originally sound units / originally sound units outside the justified change set. Predeclare sentence/paragraph unit, inspect meaning and raw diffs, report counts. Legitimate propagation is not over-editing |
| Claim Drift | Unsupported strengthening of scope, causality, completion, or generalization over successive revisions; report each occurrence and retained necessary qualifications |
| Human effort and preference | Active reading/editing time, repeated explanations, unnecessary waits, and author preference with reasons. Tokens and wall time do not substitute for human effort |

Reader comprehension and source-backed factual accuracy remain outcome checks. Blind and counterbalance final artifact comparisons; graders inspect sources, while fresh reader questions use only the document. Include unfinished tasks and budget overruns. Do not collapse severe factual errors into a style-weighted score. Allow mixed and null findings.

## Acceptance and stopping

Behavioral acceptance: explicit scope is respected, accepted facts propagate or remaining dependencies are clearly flagged, unrelated locked wording survives, and the current draft can be recovered. No false “saved”, “approved”, or “consistent final” status. A single critical conflict can fail a task even if most checks pass.

Effectiveness requires evidence across real long documents and actual author sessions, compared with ordinary iterative writing. Short synthetic regressions do not establish this. Stop after the predeclared cases; broaden only for a concrete unresolved failure. Do not keep revising cases until the Skill wins.
