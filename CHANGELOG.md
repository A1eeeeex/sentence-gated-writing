- Added compact recovery state without per-sentence files or repeated polling. Missing accepted wording must be recovered rather than reconstructed.
- Preserved paragraph intent, fact/logic/expression checks, exact accepted wording, paragraph/section review, latest-version review, and stopping rules.
- Replaced project-specific checklist examples with synthetic, portable examples.
- Expanded evaluation design to include ordinary iterative writing with comparable human feedback. No comparative improvement claims are made.
