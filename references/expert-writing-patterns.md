# Technical writing patterns

These are fallible writing heuristics, not expert theory or evaluation results. The user reports expert edits to the front portions of two reports (scenario-library framing and driving-evaluation background/route). Those paired originals and edits were not available for inspection in this update. The patterns below operationalize the supplied summary; they are not independently extracted findings or quoted project text. There is no expert gold version of the full simulation chapter available here.

| Starting material | Useful organization | Source boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A list of problems | Identify their relationship or a real technical tension, then motivate the documented method | Offer a tentative interpretation for author calibration if the relation is inferred; not every list contains a contradiction |
| Module names | Explain each role and how outputs, inputs or responsibilities connect | Distinguish serial, parallel, alternative and independent work; never invent integration |
| Actions performed | Organize relevant purpose, inputs, engineering actions and concrete output | Add only supplied details; no mandatory four-clause sentence |
| A method capability | Explain capability, boundary and the resulting need for another step | A boundary does not prove a particular next method is implemented or uniquely necessary |
| Full project ambition | Identify what this phase actually designed, ran or measured | Planned goal, demonstration and validated performance remain different |
| Repeated project thread | Give background its motivation, route its relationships, and conclusion its completion status | Repeat only the information needed at that level, not the full same explanation |
| Vague conservative language | Narrow scope and state the supported action directly | Preserve genuine uncertainty; a purpose statement is not an effectiveness result |

Use these patterns to propose a paragraph skeleton and usable prose. For example, ratings plus motion measurements may motivate investigating their relationship, if that is the author's goal. The same notes do not establish prediction, objective evaluation and fusion as three implemented routes. Invite correction through the current candidate, update the skeleton, and carry the accepted meaning into later prose.
